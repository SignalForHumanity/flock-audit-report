Flock audit report

Summarize the Flock license-plate-reader search audits you got through a public-records request: who searched, from which state, with what reasons, and how many searches mention immigration or protests. Works with files of millions of rows that Excel cannot open.

Your files stay on this device. They are read by this page in your browser and never uploaded. The page makes no network requests after it loads, and works offline.

Files saved from Excel as .xlsx must be saved as CSV first. Officer names and plate numbers are never shown; when a file has them, the report gives counts only. Reasons are shown as written (plate-like words masked) and may name people: read them before you publish. Keyword flags are leads to check, not proof.

Open source (MIT or Apache-2.0): source and documentation.
